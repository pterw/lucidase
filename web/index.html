⚠️ Scientific Scope & Guardrail: Absence of a Finding ≠ Absence of Risk

GenomePulse tests only targeted single-nucleotide polymorphisms interrogated by consumer arrays. It does not perform full gene sequencing, does not analyze unprobed exonic or intronic regions, and cannot detect large copy-number variations (CNVs) or structural repeats. Absence of a finding must never imply absence of genetic risk.
