Drop your raw DNA file here

Works with files from 23andMe, AncestryDNA and MyHeritage, and VCF files. Your file is read on this device and never uploaded.

What you get

  • A result for each place Lucidase checks, with what that result can't tell you
  • A map of where those genes sit on your chromosomes, on the Karyogram page
  • A copy you can save as a report (.md) or a table (.csv)

What it can't do

  • It is not a medical test or medical advice
  • It does not read whole genes, so a missing variant does not rule out risk
  • Markers outside its checks are counted, not explained

Technical detail:

Your results

Absence of a finding is not absence of risk.

Lucidase checks only the single positions your DNA chip read. It does not read whole genes or the regions the chip does not probe, and it cannot see larger changes such as missing or repeated stretches of DNA.

Summary

Each check Lucidase runs has one result. Each result has its own mark, so it reads without colour.

Results of the checks

Found 0 Your file has the variant this check looks for.
Not found 0 Your file was checked here and the variant was not found among the variants tested. This is not the same as 'normal'.
Not on your chip 0 Your DNA chip did not read this position, or the reading failed. No result.
Not checked 0 Markers in your file that Lucidase does not interpret. Counted only.
Unclear 0 A reading exists, but the evidence or the genotype is not clear enough to report a result.
